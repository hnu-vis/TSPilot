# LineChart composition and compilation

Visualization uses two LLM decisions on the normal path. Visual Content
Planning selects one primary host, required Insight content, interactions, and
only genuinely incompatible supporting goals. LineChart Composition binds the
fixed content to the closed source-field inventory and standard components.

`LineChartCompiler` reads authoritative request-scoped artifacts, materializes
typed records and evidence bindings, and never calculates a business result.
`LineChartValidator` enforces source, field, time, measure, unit, point-count,
Insight-coverage, and supporting-chart invariants. Invalid composition is sent
back to the second LLM stage for at most two repairs. Missing relationships are
returned through `needs_sources`; no deterministic chart fallback exists.

Screenshot/render audit is outside the publication path. Approved V4
LineCharts are persisted in full and returned to the answer as descriptors.
